🗣️ **Crash Course Hydra** by **David Vandenbogaerde**

**Hydra** is a browser-based platform for live coding visuals developed by **Olivia Jack**. The API is inspired by analog modular **synthesis**, in which multiple visual sources (oscillators, cameras, application windows, other connected windows) can be transformed, modulated, and composited via combining sequences of functions.

**David** has been developing software for about twenty years now and as the co-organizer of the CCA meetups, it should come to no surprise to you that he likes his coding creative. So when he finally sat down to play around with **Hydra** in a workshop at **C3** earlier this year, it really got under his skin. In this flash talk he'll take you through all the things Hydra he's learned in the mean time, and who knows, maybe pass on the Hydra bug.
