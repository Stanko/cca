Hello again **everyone**!

Last month we were welcomed at **[Multitude](https://www.multitude.nl/)**[ ](https://www.multitude.nl/)at their cosy office where we had a great time. We want to **thank** them for being great hosts and supporting the open source community.

But as the **summer** seems to have arrived, it is that time of year again where we can leave our buzzing laptops and beamers for what they are and head into green and sunny\\* **outdoors**. And what better place than the **Vondelpark**?

We'll be meeting in the park on **Sunday 13th** of July at **13:30.**

This time around we'd like to invite everybody to bring along a **game** that can be **played** together in the **park**. That could be a deck of **cards**, or a **frisbee**. Or maybe something completely different. Or you can just come and **hang out**. That's also totally cool.

We'll also bring a **cooler** box, some ice and some drinks, but feel free to bring along what you like.

Find us around here:
[https://www.google.com/maps/place/52%C2%B021'27.0%22N+4%C2%B052'01.2%22E/@52.3575085,4.8644147,17z/](https://www.google.com/maps/place/52%C2%B021'27.0%22N+4%C2%B052'01.2%22E/@52.3575085,4.8644147,17z/)

Hope to see you all there!
