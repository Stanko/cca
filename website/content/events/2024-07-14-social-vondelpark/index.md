Hello again everyone!

Last month, our largest crowd to date was warmly welcomed at **[Miro's](https://miro.com/)** who we want to send a **big thank you** for being wonderful hosts! Our evening program was packed as usual with inspiring talks and projects, but the evening came really to life after the talks, with everybody chatting away over a beer in the lobby...

That's why this time around, we want to get out of our bright server rooms or dark cinema parlors and move out into the bright and sunny outside for a **social event**.

We'll bring a cooler box, you bring some drinks of your choice for a good old fashioned **BYOB**. As the setting for our July gathering we'll make sure to reserve a great spot in the beautiful **Vondelpark**. We're expecting **amazing weather** for the day, but in the odd chance of rain we can seek refuge at the *'Proeflokaal 't Blauwe Theehuis'* also in the park.

So to sum it all up for the next meetup:
**Sunday 14th of July - 13:00** (1pm), **Social event** (BYOB) **@Vondelpark**

The exact location will be shared here and on the Discord the day of the event.

Hope to see you all there!
And/or online, on **[our Discord](https://discord.gg/eJJvn3487M)**
