Hello again creative coding enthusiasts!

While we're still **buzzing** from last edition, we've already got the next one ready for you, all hot and fresh.

This time around we'll be crossing the water and head into **Noord** where we're invited over at the creative agency **[Multitude](https://www.multitude.nl/)**. We're looking forward to exploring yet another new corner of the city, but what we're really excited about this time around is what we'll be talking about there.

**[Aidan Wyber](https://aidanwyber.com/)**, who is officially *the* creative coder over at Multitude, will be introducing the brand new **open-source creative tool** **p5Brandlab**, built with p5js and on top of all the hard earned brand-building experience Aidan gained working real-world client projects at Multitude.

p5Brandlab is an open-source framework for fellow creative coders to create visual & dynamic **brand identities**. And this all wrapped up in a neat and tidy client-proof UI. We're very excited to see what you all can make with it!

After the presentation, we'll be opening up the floor for a good old fashion themed **show-and-tell**. The theme? You've guessed it! **Tools**!! The theme is more of a possible inspriation source rather than a guideline, so please don't be shy! :)

Hope to see you there!
