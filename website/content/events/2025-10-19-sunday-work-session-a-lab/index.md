Hello there lovely creatives, coders and enthusiasts!

We absolutely enjoyed last month's event at Contact and want to **thank everyone** who was there and helped make it the **inspiring** evening that it was!

As for October's schedule, we're slowing down on the talks and making time for another **Sunday Working Session**.

On Sunday, **October 19th,** we're welcomed back at **[A-Lab](https://www.a-lab.nl/)** from **13:00** to **17:00**, where we'll be taking over the **[Playground](https://www.a-lab.nl/spaces#playground)** downstairs. We'll set it up so there's space for **working** as much as there is space for working as much as there is for connecting and sharing ideas.

During the **second half,** we'll also open up the stage for a **show & tell.** Anyone who wants to show their work on the **big screen** is more than welcome to do so.

This time around there are no code challenges nor themes, just a **fun weekend get-together**. And who knows, maybe you'll get some work done.

Or finally make a dent in that problem.

Or just come hang out and find out what everyone else is up to.

Either way, we bet it'll be a great time.

Hope to see you all there!

Or if not, online on our **[Discord](https://discord.gg/eJJvn3487M)**
