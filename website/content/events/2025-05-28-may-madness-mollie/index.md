As usual, we'd like to **thank** everyone who joined us for the **Plotter Special** last month! Seeing everybody huddled around the plotters was really something...

This month **May**, as we're invited back at our friends at **Mollie**'s we tried to make it even **Madder**.

Which is why, we've got not 1, not 2, but **3 amazing speakers** for you this time around!

In alphabetical order, we start off with **Giedrius Tamulaitis**, who's bringing along a peculiarly **doomed** rotary **phone**.

Continuing in alphabetical order, we've got **Masa**, who will take us on a tour through the **interactive pixel-art** wonder worlds he creates.

And finally, last but not least, still in alphabetical order, we are welcoming **Piter Pasma** to the stage. He's going to tell us a bit about his work on his "**Rayhatcher**" algorithm and the use, **beauty** and **magic** of **SDFs**.

Are you excited? Because we sure are!
We hope to see you there! Or if you can't make it, come hang out on **[our Discord](https://discord.gg/qrrKkaM4w3)**.

⚠️ As we've got a packed and exciting program we kindly ask you to try to be **punctual** so we can get an early start.

**📅 Program Details:**

**☎️ Doom Phone** - Coder and artist **[Giedrius Tamulaitis](https://tamulaitis.lt/)** will tell us a story about turning an old rotary phone into a wireless controller that can be used to play Doom. For no good reason. And you will be able to try it out!

🐰 **Interactive Pixel Art** - Creative Coder **[Masa](https://ma5a.com/)**[ ](https://ma5a.com/)used to draw things like bunnies pretending to be an ice cream, and these days he codes things like dancing meerkats. He will be telling the story of how he started creative coding and share some of his work and the processes behind them.

**☀️ Rayhatcher / SDFs - [Piter Pasma](https://piterpasma.nl/)** is a generative artist from Groningen, the Netherlands, who creates three-dimensional artworks using compact mathematical code. Known for his "[Rayhatcher](https://fxwho.xyz/fxhash/urh/)" algorithm—line-art-inspired raymarching optimized for plotters. Pasma also fosters creative coding communities through initiatives like [Genuary ](https://genuary.art/)and the collaborative Universal Rayhatcher project. In his presentation he will talk about his "Rayhatcher" algorithm and the use, beauty and magic of SDFs.
