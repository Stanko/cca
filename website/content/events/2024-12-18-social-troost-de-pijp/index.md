As the year comes to an end we want to wrap it up with a relaxed **social** event.

No presentations, laptops or beamers, *just* a bunch of fun and interesting **people getting together** for a chat an a drink.

We'll be meeting on **Wednesday 18th** of **December** at the **Brouwerij Troost**, **de Pijp**.

Even though this time around there's no agenda's nor themes, we'd love to see you there and **hear** all about how the last year was for you and what you'd like to see for **2025**. Be it about personal plans, the world, or from these meetups...

It's now almost been a year that we've been organizing these and we want to take this time to collect some **feedback** from you and discuss some of the ideas we have for he future.

So we’d **love** to see you there!

If you can’t make it this time, we still want to hear your thoughts and ideas!
That’s why we prepared [this little form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSegmjMWkxoHs5BZNkybD43oFaKFioJfcRhUIzWobUXaiKojXA/viewform).
It would be really great if you filled it out and help us shape how next year looks like for CCA.
I mean… If you’ve read this far, I’d say you care at least a little bit how that’ll look like, right? Well, here’s your chance… we promise we will listen!

And we’re still looking for a better banner! Hit us up over on [our discord](https://discord.gg/qrrKkaM4w3)
