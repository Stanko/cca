**Hello there all again!**

We had a great time last month at VolksHotel and would like to thank everyone who joined us there. It was really amazing meeting all of you.

During that session, we asked around why people were joining and what everybody was looking for in these creative coding meetups. Two of the topics that kept popping up were '**show & tell**' and '**collaborations**', so that's what we'll be trying to accommodate for our next gathering.

We'll start off with a round of quick **introductions** for everyone, with the option to hook up your laptop to a **big screen** and share what you're working on. We want to keep these very short (5-10mins), to make sure everyone gets a chance to present something.

After this first part, there is the space and time to **discuss** some details on a laptop screen, **hang out** and have a **drink**. We have a couple of different corners and tables available at the location so there should be room for everybody.

There's also a big cinema screen, so if you ever wanted to see your creations on a really large screen, this is your chance! :)

See you there!
