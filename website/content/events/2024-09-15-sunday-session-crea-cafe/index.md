**Hello again creative coders and enthusiasts!**

Last month we had an inspiring and insightful gathering in the lobby of the VolksHotel. **Thank you** to everybody that joined us there!

It was our first time exploring the **work-session** format, but we liked it so much that we've got more of this coming for september. The same (slightly revised) formula, in a new location.

For our next event we're going to **CREA Cafe** on **Sunday the 15th of September** from **13:00 to 16:00**. We will be gathering in the room to the left of the bar named 'de woonkamer', a little bit separated from the main area.

We will start the afternoon with a quick round of introductions, after which there is some time and space for **working/socialising** and towards the end we plan to do a mini **show & tell**.

Looking forward to see you there!
