Hello again curious creatives!

Have you ever wondered if you could make visual **art** through a simple set of **rules**? Well, you're in luck because we're organizing a **free workshop** to show you exactly how!

On Sunday 23rd of **November** we meet at [A-Lab](https://a-lab.nl) for a **beginner workshop** into **creative coding** guided by **[Sinan Asciolagu](https://openprocessing.org/user/1)**. In his super-duper-crash-p5-workshop he'll take you through the few basic programming concepts to recreate your own version of some of the generative artworks from the pioneers like Vera Molnar, Georg Nees, Herbert Franke.

The workshop is hands on, so **do** bring your laptop! Throughout the workshop Sinan will also be joined by community members to help people out 1-on-1 when you get stuck.

This will also be an opportunity to join our monthly meetup of our group, Creative Coding Amsterdam. So for the more **experienced** crowd, we're keeping it interesting by inviting **everyone** to **recode** and/or **reimagine** any existing artwork! This can be based in any medium. Whatever inspires you!

After the workshop we'll have the space for a **show and tell**, where everyone is welcomed to share their results and lessons for this coding workout. We're already excited!

And hope to see you all there!

Or if not, online on our **[Discord](https://discord.gg/eJJvn3487M)**

We want to wrap this up with a shout-out to the **[Amsterdam Indie Game Developers](https://www.meetup.com/amsterdam-indie-game-developers/)** for the collab last month, and a big thank you to everyone for joining and sharing their work!
