Hello again creative coders and enthusiasts!

Summer is here, and that means it's time to close the laptops, unplug the beamers, and simply **get together** for a drink and a chat.

No presentations, no themes, no agenda. *Just* a bunch of fun and interesting **people getting together** to talk about code, art, projects, plans, or whatever that's on your mind.

We'll be meeting on **Sunday, 19th of July** from **13:00** onwards. As always, everyone is welcome, and it's free to join.

We've picked a spot right on the water at **[Hannekes Boom](https://www.hannekesboom.nl/)**, a stone's throw from Centraal and NEMO. Grab a seat on the terrace, enjoy the sun, and come find us.

Come share how your year has been going, what you're working on, and what you'd like to see from these meetups. Or just come **hang out**.

So we'd **love** to see you there!

And/or online, on our [**Discord**](https://discord.gg/eJJvn3487M).
