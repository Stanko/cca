Hello again **creative coders** and enthusiasts!

We hope you all had warm and joyful **holidays** while the weather has been anything but. Luckily, it is also a time with plenty of reasons to stay indoors.

One of those reasons that has been keeping us warm and busy in **2025** so far, is **[Genuary](https://genuary.art)**. For those unfamiliar, genuary is 'an artificially generated month of time where we build code that makes beautiful things'. Or 31 prompts, one for each day of **January**, meant to inspire and tickle your creative coding itch.

So that is why, for our next meetup we'll have **'Genuary'** as the **theme**, while as format we're mixing things up a little bit again.

This time we're having a **mix** of a **social event** and a **group-work session**. There will be space for laptops, so you can get some work done (right in time before Genuary ends :) or show off your creations or something you've seen online... Bringing a laptop is totally optional though. So if you just want to hang out for a chat and see what everyone's up to that's perfectly cool as well.

We've reserved the long tables next to the bar in **CREA Cafe**, to accomodate our format and we'll also make sure there's **wifi** available this time around.

**Hope to see you all there!**
