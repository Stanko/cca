On the **9th of May**, we're joining forces with the [Amsterdam Indie Game Developers](https://www.meetup.com/amsterdam-indie-game-developers/) for a full afternoon and evening at [A Lab](https://www.a-lab.nl/en).

One big room. Two communities.  
**Games, art, code**, and the people who make them.

We’ll be filling the space with **exhibition** and **play-test** booths. Indie games in the making you can play, creative projects to interact with, and the makers right there to talk to. It's going to be informal and a bit chaotic in the best possible way. Come for the work, stay for the people.

Towards the evening we'll gather for a round of short **talks** and **showcases** from both communities before we wind things down.

We've got a great mix of people signed up already!  
Want to join them and show your work?  
There's still space! → [forms.cca.codes/may](https://forms.cca.codes/may)

All are welcome.  
Free to attend.

Hope to see you there! 💜
