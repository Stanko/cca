Hello lovely creatives, coders and enthusiasts!

We're back with another relaxed **Cowork Session**. The weather's still nice, but we're guessing you're also itching to get back to your projects.

Join us **Sunday, July 30th** from **13:00**, at **[Contact Maker Space](https://www.contactamsterdam.nl/)**. Whether you want to work on a personal project, finally finish that one sketch or simply hang out, this is the perfect afternoon for it. Bring your laptop, bring your ideas and let's create together in a relaxed environment. Share knowledge, ask questions or just get some quiet coding done

There's no pressure to show anything off, but if you feel like sharing what you're working on, we're always happy to take a look and swap ideas. If everything goes right, we'll get a few "ooh, what's that?" moments.

Hope to see you there!

Or if not, catch us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eJJvn3487M).
