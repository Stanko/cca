[RSTR](https://rstr.d17e.dev/) by David is an online tool that turns your images and videos into plottable SVG's.

[The Vera Molnar prompt](https://genuary.d17e.dev/?prompt=5) for Genuary 2024 was the direct inspiration for this project.
