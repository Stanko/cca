What it is

An informal meetup built around games in progress, creative projects, and the people making them.

On Saturday, May 9, 2026, both communities take over one shared room at A Lab for a full afternoon and evening of showing work, trying things out, and talking shop.

Expect exhibition and play-test booths, interactive projects, and a loose, lively atmosphere with makers right there to talk about what they are building.

Program

Daytime hands-on, evening show-and-tell.

Daytime

Exhibition + play testing

Move between booths, play indie games in the making, interact with community projects, and meet the people behind the work.

Evening

Talks + show and tell

Later on, everyone gathers for a round of short talks, showcases, and quick presentations from both communities before the night winds down.

Come for the work, stay for the people.

Assembly is a joined effort by Creative Coding Amsterdam and Amsterdam Indie Game Developers. By community for community.
